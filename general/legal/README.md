# Legal document drafts (Brickspace Lab)

This folder contains **draft** terms and policies for Brickspace Lab’s website and products (**Slab**, **Catalog**). These documents are **not legal advice** and have **not** been reviewed by counsel. You should have a qualified lawyer in Ontario review and customize them before publication—especially company legal name, registered address, pricing model, refund stance, subprocessors, and insurance limits.

## Placeholders to replace

Search for `[BRACKETS]` in each file and replace with your finalized details:

- `[LEGAL_ENTITY_NAME]` — e.g. Brickspace Lab Inc.
- `[REGISTERED_ADDRESS]` — complete Ontario business address
- `[CONTACT_EMAIL]` — legal/privacy contact
- `[SUPPORT_EMAIL]` — general support
- `[WEBSITE_URL]` — primary marketing site
- `[SUBPROCESSORS]` — Stripe, hosting, email, analytics (keep list current)
- `[LAST_UPDATED]` — date of publication or revision

## Relationship between documents

- **Terms of service** is the umbrella agreement for the website and services; it incorporates or links to the **privacy policy**, **refund policy**, **acceptable use policy**, and product **EULAs** where applicable.
- **EULAs** govern license scope for Slab and Catalog specifically (non-transferable use, IP, termination).
- **Cookie consent** copy should align with what you actually deploy (banner tool, categories of cookies, opt-in where required).
