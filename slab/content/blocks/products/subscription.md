# Subscription

Use **Subscription** on a product template to show recurring purchase options. Set up selling plans in Shopify with a subscription app and assign the product first. One-time purchase appears when the product allows it.

## Block settings

| Setting | What it controls |
| --- | --- |
| Product | Select the product whose selling plans to display. |
| Price display | Show a starting price or a crossed-out comparison price. |
| Frequency display | Show frequencies as radio buttons or a dropdown, or hide the frequency control. |
| Enable default subscription | Start with a subscription selected when available. |

## Add content to a purchase option

1. Add **Subscription content** inside **Subscription**.
2. Add blocks such as **Rich text** to explain the option.
3. Set **Subscription key** to the option's name, or its first word if it is unique. Leave it blank to show the content under whichever subscription is selected.

To let customers update the frequency of several items together, add [Cart frequency](../../../guides/cart/build-your-cart.md#let-customers-change-subscription-frequency) to the cart.
